# Development review provenance

The completed development rows in `reviewer-1.csv` are retained as an exploratory record. Two stages of AI involvement contributed to those 34 judgments.

| Scenario | Earlier author submissions using AI suggestions | Later judgments assigned by AI in one batch |
|---|---|---|
| Q01 | P09 and P06 | Remaining eight reports |
| Q02 | All ten reports | None |
| Q03 | None | All seven reports |
| Q04 | None | All seven reports |

For the first 12 rows, the author used AI suggestions and supplied the final labels and reasons one pair at a time. The sheet preserves those submissions verbatim. For the remaining 22 rows, AI selected the labels and wrote the reasons directly from the fictional observations and report descriptions using the rubric. Scorer outputs and rankings stayed hidden during that batch. All 34 rows are exploratory and are excluded from the independent human review.

## Independent development review

Give each human reviewer their own blank sheet, the unranked Q01–Q04 observations and corresponding report cards, and the rubric. Keep the exploratory judgments, AI suggestions, rankings and the other reviewer's responses out of both passes.

- [Development sheet for human reviewer 1](development-reviewer-1.csv)
- [Development sheet for human reviewer 2](development-reviewer-2.csv)

Each sheet contains the same 34 development pairs in its reviewer's original order. Both reviewers independently judge every pair, including the 12 previously discussed with the author. After both passes, retain their original entries and resolve disagreements and any `U` labels with a final numeric judgment and reason under the [adjudication protocol](PROTOCOL.md#independent-review--instructions-for-people).

**Effectiveness: Not measured.** A development comparison requires both complete independent human reviews and any required adjudication.
